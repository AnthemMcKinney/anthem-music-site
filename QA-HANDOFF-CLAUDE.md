# Anthem Music beta: independent release QA handoff

**For Claude:** Please review this as an independent QA tester, not as a continuation of the implementation. Challenge assumptions, reproduce issues, and prioritize defects that could hurt a student's lesson or a public Android release. Do not start new feature work. Return a concise release-risk report with reproducible findings and a device test matrix.

## Product and goals

Anthem Music is a responsive web app/PWA with tuner, metronome, first-chord lessons, and a More page. It teaches students to tune, hear chords, follow strumming, and understand chord movement rather than memorize isolated songs. The current beta is **3.7.24** (More page); cache revision is **3.7ax**. The immediate plan is one week of student/parent field testing, then fixes and a Google Play submission. The target is a stable Play Store release in about a month. This week is for polish and correctness of existing modules, not new features. A native Android beta has existed, but this pass updates the website/PWA only; the APK must be rebuilt separately before Android package QA. iOS is web only for now.

Longer term, the owner may add more instrument curricula and optional privacy-conscious usage/crash analytics, and eventually pursue iOS distribution. No login is planned for the first public release. Do not introduce accounts, data collection, or new teaching modules as part of this audit.

## Changes in beta 3.7.24 that need independent testing

1. Metronome beat 1 now uses the selected theme accent and a quick expanding glow. Starting the metronome on iPhone without audio priming should flash the Sound Check button green and give a clear instruction. Test repeated Start attempts, failed/retried Sound Check, background/resume, mute, sound choices, subdivisions, meter choices, and reduced-motion settings. Confirm beat 1 is distinct in all five themes and the glow never obscures its numeral.
2. The tuner still requires three distinct good plucks, but onset and center-zone acceptance were relaxed. Previously the user saw frequent green-zone clicks yet only about one of every three or four plucks advanced the solid green confirmation line. Test with real instruments and microphones on Android/iPhone, both quiet and noisy rooms. Count whether each genuinely separate, reasonably centered pluck advances exactly once; sustained sound must not count multiple times. Verify far/sharp/flat pitches never pass and switching strings/tunings resets the count. The gauge's strict center/ding condition remains separate from the slightly wider qualifying zone.
3. Responsive rules were adjusted so the tuner shows every string in phone landscape and coarse-pointer tablet landscape; the lesson content can scroll vertically; four-measure practice boxes should not require horizontal scrolling. Landscape expanded-list text is larger. Audit layout at real device scale and browser zoom/text scaling, not only emulated viewport dimensions.
4. A single expandable **Requested Practice** area was added to the Practice tab for each instrument family (both General and Young uke paths). Guitar requests moved out of Objective 2/3: `Am–G–Em–D` and `Em–Em–C–B7`. Standard uke requests are `C–Em–F–C` and a slow, tap-backed `F F F F | C7 C7 F F` (Mary Had a Little Lamb). The latter should start at 68 BPM, with no drum groove. Baritone has four two-measure phrases based on the owner's simplified *Remember Me* sequence: `C | Fm`, `C | B♭ B♭ E7 E7`, `Am | C7`, `F | Fm Fm G7 G7`. Phrases are 72 BPM with taps, and include playable new Fm, B♭, and C7 shapes. Test chord diagrams and audio pitch against DGBE baritone tuning. This is an exercise arrangement, not a verified bar-for-bar licensed transcription of the film song; confirm it works against the student's specific melody before treating it as exact.
5. Active strum arrows and numbered-beat rest dots should keep fixed size/position when highlighted; only weight/glow changes. Beat numerals and “&” counts must never disappear. Check this specifically on iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, and the Android wrapper.

## Professional QA coverage requested

Please produce a matrix that covers all **five themes × four modules × five viewport classes** (phone portrait/landscape, tablet portrait/landscape, desktop). Use representative real devices as well as responsive browser tools. Include iOS Safari/PWA, Android Chrome and installed Android APK when a new APK exists, and desktop Chrome/Edge. Rotate while audio is playing and while an accordion/popup is open. Check safe areas, browser chrome, system text scale, portrait/landscape transitions, on-screen keyboard, pinch zoom, and reduced-motion preference. A person with older eyes should be able to read controls and expanded instructions without horizontal scrolling or edge clipping.

For every theme, inspect contrast and palette consistency on headers, tuner strings/frets, selected buttons, metronome beat indicators, sound controls, chord diagrams, chord popups, strum/arrows, exercise tabs, Requested Practice, and More accordions. Look for old blue/orange artifacts where the chosen theme should govern. Check hover, focus, pressed, disabled, playing, in-tune, and error states. Verify icons and plus/minus marks are centered and touch targets are usable. Check focus order, keyboard operation, screen-reader names/status, labels on icons, and whether visual-only cues have a text alternative.

**Tuner:** Test all listed instruments and tunings, including guitar, ukulele variants, bass/violin/other available choices. Confirm all strings remain selectable in landscape, fret/string lines remain visible on dark themes, selected-row emphasis is subtle, reference notes sound, mic permissions and denied/revoked permissions recover, auto-detect and manual selection behave, and the three-pluck confirmation is neither too strict nor falsely permissive. Test low-volume plucks, fast repeated plucks, ringing strings, wrong octave, changing instrument mid-session, and leaving the module while mic is active.

**Metronome:** Test 40/maximum BPM and tempo changes while running, tap tempo, each subdivision/meter/sound, first-beat accent, rock groove (closed hats with open fourth hat, kick 1/3, snare 2/4), pause/resume, background/foreground, and audio unlock on iOS. Watch for visual/audio drift over several minutes, doubled beats after orientation changes, and click/glow animation restarting correctly every measure. Try Start before Sound Check, then prime it, and retry after a failed audio permission/autoplay attempt.

**Chord lessons/practice:** Walk every instrument, path, objective, and Practice tab. Expand/collapse notes, exercise buttons, progression cards and Requested Practice in different orders; switch tabs while playback runs. Test chord audio, popups, alternates, accurate string/fret labels, the fixed-position play button, and sound enable on iPhone. Follow Along should repeat until stopped, stop when navigating away, highlight the correct arrow or skipped numbered downbeat, and keep the counts fixed. Test custom tempo entry, step controls, drum toggle for all general Practice progressions, immediate rhythm-locked switch to taps, and tap-backed requested examples. Check the two-measure mixed-chord bars beat by beat. Observe for chord/beat timing lag, dropouts, overlapping audio, audio continuing after navigation, and controls becoming visually stuck. Make sure no four-box layout clips or requires horizontal scroll in landscape.

**More/PWA/release:** Verify appearance selection persists, version 3.7.24 is visible, privacy and recording credits are accurate, external links and feedback work, service-worker cache updates actually deliver new assets, offline launch behavior is reasonable, and there are no console errors or missing samples. Check install/update behavior and Android Back navigation. The eventual Play Store submission also needs separate package/version code, manifest/permissions, icon, screenshots, target-SDK, signing, privacy disclosure, and store-listing QA; website QA alone cannot certify it.

Probe stress cases: very narrow and very wide screens, 200% browser zoom, large OS text, slow or offline network, blocked audio/mic, headphones/Bluetooth handoff, device sleep/wake, repeated rapid taps, and returning from another app. Watch for memory or CPU growth during extended playback. Check whether a first-time student can tell where to start and what each control does without teacher explanation.

## Reporting format

Report **findings first**, ordered by release severity (blocker, high, medium, low). For each, provide exact steps, expected/actual result, device/browser/theme/orientation, frequency, screenshot or short recording if possible, and file/line if a code cause is evident. Separate reproduced defects from hypotheses and physical-device tests you could not run. Include a pass/fail/not-tested matrix, accessibility concerns, audio timing observations, and a clear “ready for student field test / ready for public release” judgment with reasons. Recommend the smallest focused fixes; avoid broad redesigns or new features during this stabilization period.
