THE MUSICAL ALPHABETA · B · C · D · E · F · G · A

Natural notes use the seven letter names. After G, the alphabet begins again at A in a higher octave. Sharps and flats name the pitches between most natural notes. B–C and E–F sit directly next to each other with no note between them.
