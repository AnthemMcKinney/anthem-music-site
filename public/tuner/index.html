A little help tuning

Find the note: pluck about once each second and make small turns until you hear the ding. The ding means you are close, not finished.

Do the five-pluck check: pluck five more times and watch the trail. If most readings drift to one side, make one tiny adjustment and count five again. Move on when the five plucks average around the center line.

If you are not sure where to start: tap the string to hear its note. Match it by ear, then turn Auto Detect back on to finish.

Stop tightening if a string feels unusually tight. Check the selected instrument, tuning, and string before continuing.
